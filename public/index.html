Latest Event

Event Details

FX 13 VALOUR ROBOTICS CLUB presents an exciting competition "ROBOTICS WITHOUT BORDERS" combining Engineering, Robotics, and Presentation for students of Classes 5–6 and Classes 7–8. The event will be conducted in two competitive rounds, with participants competing individually (Solo). In Round 1, participants will take an on-site pen-and-paper examination designed to test their speed, accuracy, and foundational knowledge of Mathematics, Engineering and Physics principles, and Robotics fundamentals. The top 8 participants will advance to Round 2, where they will participate in an online presentation through Zoom and showcase a project or solution based on a designated theme connecting robotics and environmental sustainability, such as automated waste management and global cleanup solutions. Event information, announcements, resources, and Zoom details will be provided through the official club website, while finalists will receive further instructions through email. The competition will recognize the top three participants as Champion, First Runner-Up, and Second Runner-Up, with official certificates awarded in recognition of their achievements.

Category

Category Junior: Class 5 and 6

Category Senior: Class 7 and 8

Event Format

The event will be conducted in two rounds. Round 1 will be an on-site written examination consisting of 10 MCQs and 1 free response question, covering Mathematics, Engineering & Physics, and Robotics fundamentals, with a total of 25 marks. The top 8 participants will advance to Round 2, an online presentation and project display conducted via Zoom. Participants may use any number of slides but must complete their presentation within a strict 8-minute time limit, focusing on a designated theme combining robotics and environmental sustainability.

Example Questions

Question 1: A cylindrical water tank has a radius of 7 cm and a height of 20 cm. A robotic system fills the tank to 75% of its capacity. If the robot pumps water at a rate of 11 cm³ per second, approximately how many seconds will it take to fill the tank to that level?
(Take π = 22/7)
A) 150 s B) 175 s C) 210 s D) 220 s
Answer: B) 175s Question 2: Which component of a robot is primarily responsible for detecting changes in the robot's surroundings, such as distance, light, temperature, or obstacles?
A) Actuator B) Sensor C) Motor D) Microcontroller
Answer: B) Sensor

PATHFINDERS
Class 3 & 4 Solo

Designed for students of Classes 3, 4, and 5, Pathfinders is an exciting challenge that blends logic, problem-solving, and the fundamentals of coding into one engaging experience. Participants will be presented with a grid-based puzzle where a robot must navigate from its starting point to its destination avoiding obstacles along the way. Using a sequence of simple commands such as "MOVE FORWARD" and "TURN RIGHT" or "TURN LEFT", students will “program” the robot’s path, just like real world coding. With increasing levels of difficulty across classes larger grids, more obstacles, and limited command spaces students must think critically, plan efficiently, and execute these commands on paper precisely. The Top Three participants who complete the challenge with the most accurate solutions in the shortest time will be crowned winners! Pathfinders is more than just a competition it’s a journey into the world of programmable thinking, where creativity meets logic and every move counts.

Our Projects

AeroSense AQ-1

Aerosense AQ-1 is Valour's finest projects. It is a UAV purpose built for collecting air pollution data. Built from cutting edge, high precision technology the Aerosense is able to record the concentration of air pollutants (such as CO2, SO2) in our atmosphere. AQ-1 is Valour's first steps towards real time sensing, navigation and automated aerial vehicles. We, the innovators of GreenHerald Int. School, aim to develop our society and work for the well being of both our planet and our communities.
